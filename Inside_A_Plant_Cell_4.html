About this project

This website was made in accordance with the Cell Model project. It lets you explore a plant cell by tapping its parts, looking inside them, comparing plant and animal cells, running a photosynthesis simulation, and testing yourself with a quiz.

Illustrations are simplified drawings based on electron microscope images and textbook diagrams. Sizes are typical ranges.
